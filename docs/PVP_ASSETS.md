# PvP assets (CX-20)

Every texture and animation added for PvP mode, the meters and fighting styles, with its size and where it is used. All of them are original and generated by tools in this repo, so any of them can be replaced by hand-drawn art at the same size and path. How the pieces fit together is in [PVP_GUIDE.md](PVP_GUIDE.md).

Notes:
- **Replacing a generated texture:** remove its call from `tools/ArtGen.java` (`MeterArt.all()` or the painter in `NpcArt.masters()`), or the next ArtGen run overwrites it.
- **GUI textures (4x scale):** the meter textures are drawn at four times its on-screen size (4 texture pixels per GUI pixel), like the Saga HUD. Keep that ratio when replacing one.
- **Masters' hair and parts:** hair, antennae, a tail, a cape and the like aren't in the skins. They are 3D parts from `client/render/NpcLooks.java`.
- **Spawn eggs:** they use vanilla's template (`models/item/<master>_spawn_egg.json`), coloured by `MasterRoster`.
- **Crosshairs:** the star and the dot are drawn in code (`client/ui/Crosshair`), not from textures.

| `fight_walk` | stride 2.0 blocks, symmetric | The default fighting steps |
