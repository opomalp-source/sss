# World structures, civilizations and building blocks

Progress document for the user's request of 2026-10-22: make the world look and feel like the Dragon Ball universe
with recognizable canon locations, believable alien civilizations, original Dragon Ball-style places, custom building
blocks per civilization, furnished interiors, and settlements the living world's NPCs (`docs/LIVING_WORLD.md`), the
Nemesis system (`docs/NEMESIS.md`) and the combat AI (`docs/COMBAT_AI.md`) actually use. Keep this current: the work
spans several releases.

## 1. Audit (v0.163.0)

- **Platform:** Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47 (NeoForge legacy toolchain), Java 17.
- **Dimensions** (`data/dbzenith/dimension`): Earth (overworld), `namek` (custom biomes and Namekian dome houses,
  `world/NamekHouseFeature`, vanilla blocks), `northern_planet` and `otherworld`/`limbo` (built once in code by
  `OtherworldBuilder`: check-in station, Snake Way, King Kai's planet, the Grand Kai's island, Limbo),
  `beerus_planet` (`BeerusPlanetBuilder`: a stepped violet pyramid temple, the Oracle Fish pond), `time_chamber`
  (a white void). The galaxy (`galaxy/`) adds procedural planet instances; the series' worlds Planet Vegeta, Frieza
  No. 79, Yardrat and Cereal (`IconicSystems`) get a small plaza of vanilla blocks at their landing site
  (`IconicLandmarks`).
- **Earth landmarks** (`world/landmark`, CX-33): not vanilla structures but deterministic plans per site
  (`LandmarkSites`: one per `spacing` cell, biome and terrain checked, read from the terrain generator without making
  chunks), drawn chunk by chunk through `Canvas` by one feature (`LandmarkFeature`, biome modifier `landmarks`): the
  World Martial Arts Tournament, Kami's Lookout with Korin Tower, the Cell Games arena, the Frypan Mountains. West City
  is a placeholder that is never generated. No protection for already explored worlds (a new landmark could build up
  against old terrain).
- **Blocks:** the landmark set (`world/arch`, 49 bases plus stairs, slabs and walls) generated by
  `tools/gen_architecture.py` (Python, not installed on this machine), style: 16x16, flat anime colours, a soft grain,
  one-pixel bevels lit from the top left, darker joints. Everything else (houses, Namek, other planets) is vanilla.
- **NPCs and settlements:** the living world's settlements are registered in code (`living.TownBuilder`, Ginger
  Town); residents follow points of interest; open doorways only (no door handling).
- **Loot and interactions:** quest givers, style masters, chests in places; no structure loot tables of our own.

## 3. Design

- **Block families** (`world/arch/CivilizationBlocks`, generated by `tools/BlockFamilyGen.java`, a Java port of the
  landmark generator's style so they sit together): Capsule Corporation / Earth, Saiyan, Namekian, Frieza Force,
  Divine. Cubes, top-and-bottom and pillar blocks, painted windows (round "capsule" and porthole windows), lamps,
  stairs, slabs and walls, a rounded corner piece per family (a quarter-round column model, so curved walls read as
  curved), sliding doors per family (doors NPCs open), and insignia panels drawn for the mod (the Capsule Corporation
  mark, the Saiyan royal crest, the Frieza Force mark).
- **Settlements from world generation** (`world/settle`): a settlement is a landmark plan that also lists its points
  of interest and its people. It is drawn chunk by chunk like any landmark; when a player first comes near, it is
  registered with the living world (`Settlement` with every home, shop, lab, dojo, plaza, patrol point and gate; a
  stable id from the site, so it happens once) and peopled by trade; bodies appear as their homes load. Doors and
  stairs are walkable for residents (they open doors).
- **Existing worlds:** each new landmark kind remembers which regions already existed the first time a server ran
  with it, and never builds in them; new worlds get everything.
- **Placement:** unique canon landmarks once per large cell (as before), biome and terrain checked, never overlapping
  other landmarks; modular layouts (road grids and building lots) rather than one rigid template.

## 5. Built

### v0.164.0

- **Block families** (`world/arch/CivilizationBlocks`, assets by `java tools/BlockFamilyGen.java`): 63 base blocks
  across Capsule Corporation, Saiyan, Namekian, Frieza Force and Divine, plus their stairs, slabs and walls; all with
  textures, models, blockstates, loot, tool tags and names, in their own creative tab ("Dragon Block Zenith:
  Civilizations"). Rounded corners (`RoundCorner`: a quarter-round column, its collision turning with it) and sliding
  doors (`SlidingDoor`: iron sounds, open by hand; open, it has slid into the wall and has no collision, so a crowd at
  a doorway isn't held up by a door leaf).
- **Settlements from world generation** (`world/settle`): `SettlementPlan` (a landmark plan with places, people, a
  map for long walks and a stable settlement id from its site) and `SettlementSites` (every five seconds, the nearest
  settlement landmark to each player on Earth; within reach and with its centre loaded it is registered with the
  living world once in a world's life and peopled by `Residents.settle`: residents two to a home in the plan's order,
  bodies appearing as homes load). New: the scientist trade (works in labs, the `LAB` place kind, a research goal,
  its own schedule, look and lines) and per-settlement populations (`living.settlementDensity` scales them).
- **Long walks** (`living/Routes`): a resident's path search only covers a box round it (follow range 40, 640
  steps), so heading straight for a place across a city it ended up against the far wall of whatever building it had
  walked into. A settlement now keeps a map: waypoints outdoors (along the avenues, on the lawns, the step before
  every door), its buildings as rooms (a box, or round for domes and towers, each with the waypoint at its door; a
  dome's upper floor and basement are rooms of their own, entered from the foot or top of their stairs), and which
  waypoints are joined by open ground (looked up in the world, kept, renewed daily). A walk longer than 30 blocks, or
  from one building into another, goes out by the door, waypoint to waypoint (legs of at most 36) and in by the other
  door; progress is judged on the leg. Towns without a map (Ginger Town) walk as before. Residents also leave a door
  open while anyone else is in the doorway (`DoorGoal`), and a resident whose destination changes plans afresh.
- **The existing-world guard** (`LandmarkGuard`): the first time a server runs with a guarded landmark kind, the
  overworld region files then on disk are recorded (`data/dbzenith_landmark_guard.dat`) and that kind never builds
  where its reach touches them. New worlds record nothing.
- **Unique landmarks**: one per world, the first cell (ring by ring out from 0, 0) whose site suits. The search runs
  once in a world's life (about 3 s of world generation the first time, measured) and the cell is kept in the guard's
  file, so the site never moves even if a later version changes the rules. A site's own ground is tested before its
  neighbours' plans (the costly part).
- **West City** (`world/landmark/WestCity`; unique, guarded; plains, meadow, savanna, forest, birch forest, beach;
  dry, level ground): a round city 240 blocks across levelled into the land, avenues every 36 blocks with lane lines,
  sidewalks and street lamps (on the road side, clear of doors), and lots between them:
  - **Capsule Corporation**: the great white dome (radius 17) with window rings and the company's mark on its front,
    double sliding doors under a canopy; inside the lobby with the reception desk, the atrium garden with a tree and a
    sparring ring, Dr. Brief's lab and the Capsule Room; upstairs (a stair on the east side) four family rooms; below
    (a stair down) the storage room and workshop with the time machine's pod. The lab wing (a smaller dome joined by a
    passage, with its own door onto the garden), the West Tower (a banded drum with floors and a ladder, a yellow cap),
    the Gravity Room in the garden (a white ball with portholes, the mod's gravity chamber inside), palms, a pond, open
    lawns edged with a low white kerb.
  - capsule houses (domes in five colours, two beds each; each door toward a paved road, or onto the walk between the
    rows at the city's edge), rounded three-storey apartment blocks (two flats a floor, a stair), the park with a
    fountain, a shop and restaurant on a little market, a second restaurant, the Sangokuichi Dojo (red pillars, tiled
    hip roof, punching bags), research labs, a police post. Public buildings have double doors.
  - 20 residents: 3 scientists (they live at Capsule Corporation), 3 guards (the police post), then the houses a lot
    at a time (so people live all over the city), then the flats: 2 merchants, 2 cooks, a master and 3 students, 6
    citizens.
- **Tools**: `/dbz structures list | info | places | register | refresh | resettle | path <from> <to> | rebuild
  <landmark>`. `places` checks every place of the nearest settlement: room to stand, and a resident's way there from
  the centre, walked in legs by its map as residents go. `refresh` brings a registered settlement's places and map up
  to its plan; `resettle` retires its residents and registers it afresh; `path` prints a resident's own path search
  node by node. `/landmark locate | tp | survey west_city`.
- **Tests** (`StructureTests`): blocks registered with loot, doors anyone can open, corners that turn; residents
  walking through sliding doors from either side, two at once, and on a slant from a room; long walks door to door;
  the guard (reading region files, blocking old regions, leaving others free); West City unique and stable; its
  places (homes for all, labs, shops, every kind) inside the city; settlements registered once and peopled.
- **Checked in the dev client:** West City generated in fresh ground beside explored regions (the guard kept it off
  them); `places`: all 125 places reachable; a resettled city through a morning and an evening, nobody giving up on a
  place. On the way: residents had been shut in (fences, a single-door dome, a wedge between the dome and the lab
  wing, upper floors and the basement out of the search's reach), and a crowd of residents leaving one block of flats
  stuck at its door. Fixed by the map, double doors, sliding doors without a leaf, and spreading the homes.

## 6. Not done / limits

- Milestones 2 to 7 of the plan above.
- The older landmarks (tournament, Lookout, Cell Games, Frypan) are neither unique nor guarded (as before: one per
  cell, they may generate in unexplored chunks of an old world, as they always did).
- A settlement is registered when a player first comes near; if its chunks were generated without anyone near (a
  pre-generated world), it still registers on the first visit.
- West City's interiors are furnished with vanilla furniture (beds, chests, barrels, brewing stands) and the family's
  panels; there are no Capsule Corporation machines with their own functions yet.
- Residents still give up on a place now and then (logged: "... gave up on place #n ..."); the town retries it the
  next day. A building's stair is 1 wide, and flats fill only after every house.
- The outer avenues end where the city's circle cuts them (a ragged edge on the map, though walkable).
